signature ideas

Beautiful, and kind to the planet.

  • Catering

    Cape terroir menus

    A menu built from ingredients sourced close to home, from West Coast seafood to Elgin apples.

  • Florals

    No floral foam

    Reusable mechanics like chicken wire and water tubes, so the arrangements are as responsible as they are lovely.

  • Decor

    Sensory tablescapes

    Fresh sage, rosemary and eucalyptus woven through table runners, so the scent is part of the story.

  • Keepsake

    Living centrepieces

    Potted herbs and plants that guests can take home to grow, carrying the memory of the day with them.

how to book

From first hello to the big day.

  1. Tell us about it

    Share your date, venue, guest count and any inspiration, such as a Pinterest board.

  2. Book a consultation

    We'll sit down (in person or online) to talk through your vision, menu and styling.

  3. Receive your proposal

    A tailored plan covering food, florals, decor and service, with transparent pricing.

  4. Confirm your date

    Once you're happy, we lock in your booking and start planning the details.

  5. Enjoy the day

    We take care of the setup, service and styling, so you can be a guest at your own event.
